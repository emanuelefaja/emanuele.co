When we look at a forest or a jungle, it looks so peaceful from a distance. And yet, inside this area, there will be countless species hunting and eating each other, and every second thousands of individual animals and insects will be meeting their deaths, being ripped apart or eaten alive in horrific ways.

The world looks beautiful from afar, but that’s not the case when you look at the details. 

And sometimes, the opposite is true!
