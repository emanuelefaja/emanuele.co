---
title: "Today Is All We Have."
date: 2024-04-04T07:35:19Z
categories: [decision-making, habits, short]
---

Think of your plans and goals.

It is slightly paradoxical that we can make long-term plans, but we can only experience life one day at a time, or perhaps just one moment at a time.

So today is all we have to make our future plans real. What we have to do in the future doesn’t matter; what’s important is what we decide to do today.

Then tomorrow comes, and we repeat the process.
