---
title: "Let The Arrow Fly."
date: 2022-04-03T11:47:00Z
categories: [uncategorized]
---

Unfortunately, no matter how hard we try, we are not masters of our fate. The world is a random place. Sometimes it is beautiful, sometimes cruel, but the one thing you can be sure of is that your plans will not be respected.

So what can we do?

The best way to live and stay sane in an inherently chaotic world is to be comfortable letting the arrow fly.

So practice shooting, choose the best bow and find the best conditions and time, but once you let your arrow fly, you have to know that there is nothing else you can do.

The target might move, causing you to miss, or a sudden wind may take it off course. This is none of your concern, as you have already done your part. Remember, your responsibility ends when you let go, so you should not worry about a flying arrow.

Concentrate on your next shot.
