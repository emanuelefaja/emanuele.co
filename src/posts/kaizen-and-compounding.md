As I understand it, Kaizen is all about compounding small incremental improvements.

You wake up — how can you make your life better by 1% today? Wait, perhaps even that is too much. What about 0.1%? We can all improve our lives by 1/1000th in a day.

Clean your room, learn one fact, do one pushup.
