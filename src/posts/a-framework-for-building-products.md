I came across an interesting framework by DJ Patil, the first US Chief Data Scientist, summarizing his approach to tackling problems.

![](/media/2022/11/Product-Management-Framework.jpeg)

**The framework for execution.**

-   Dream in years
-   Plan in months
-   Evaluate in weeks
-   Ship daily

**The framework for scale.**

-   Prototype for 1x
-   Build for 10x
-   Engineer for 100x

**The framework for effectiveness.**

-   What’s required to cut the timeline in 1/2?
-   What needs to be done to double the impact?

I like this — it makes intuitive sense with other things I have read and practised over the years. The idea of linking long-term dreams to daily actions can be practised both in business and our personal lives.
