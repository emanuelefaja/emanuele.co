title: "Just Ask."
date: 2022-03-18T01:12:00Z
categories: [uncategorized]
---

Those who ask, are those who will receive.

Asking questions is how you change the status quo.

Asking questions makes you think.

Asking questions makes you think “why?”.

Asking questions makes you think “why not?”.

Asking questions makes you reevaluate how things currently stand and open up a world of possibilities.

Asking questions strengthens your character. We grow up in a world where rejection is seen as harmful when in reality we should embrace it, because it is feedback, and feedback is precious. It guides us, it points us in the right direction.

There is often little to be lost in asking for something, and much to be gained.
