title: "A Few Haikus."
date: 2022-01-18T15:49:00Z
categories: [short, writing]

## 1.

when she kisses me
my love hangs in the spring air
but it is not her

## 2.

things, they keep you
still chasing at an empty life
moments that are lost

## 3.

don’t ask permission
nature’s course always runs on
but beg forgiveness

## 4.

darkness envelops
my bright light still shines bravely
while they rest, I work

## 5.

things come to an end
nothing can be permanent
smile, have a fresh start

## 6.

coffee reading
some type of people watching
this is my school day

## 7.

a pretty girl looks
over her shoulder at me
summer has arrived

## 8.

desire inside me
keeps me occupied in life o
one day it will go

## 9.

my mind runs around
thinking ten thousand thoughts,
failing every day

## 10.

mum makes a coffee
small pleasures in life are best
I am now awake!

## 11.

writing every day
my ideas develop and
change as I grow up

## 12.

each day goes past
something changes a bit
later we notice

## 13.

the past cannot change
the future is unknowable
the present is
