People often spend a lot of time and energy thinking about becoming successful and earning enough money to have a comfortable life. Very few people think deeply about what happens *after* you are successful? What are you going to do? What will make you wake up in the morning?

As I've gone through my 20s and have now turned 30, this question is something that is starting to creep up on me, I am reaching the point where money, a comfortable life, and material things are no longer objective in themselves.

So, what should one focus on after achieving the standard definition of success?
