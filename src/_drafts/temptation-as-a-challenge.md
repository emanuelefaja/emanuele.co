We all have things we want to achieve in our lives that require us to delay gratification and say no to something else we want, which is often more immediate.

I like to see these temptations as challenges. So when a pretty girl calls me on a Friday night to come out for a drink, and I’ve got boxing early on a Saturday morning, I have to say no because I want to prioritize my fitness over my social life at the moment.

This is a tough choice, but the right one if I want to achieve my goals.

I enjoy going out, *especially* in the presence of pretty girls.
