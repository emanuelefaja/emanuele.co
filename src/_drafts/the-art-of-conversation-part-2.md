## The Enduring Benefits of Mastering the Art of Conversation.

Conversation is how we build relationships in the world, and the quality and number of relationships in your life can significantly affect how you feel about your own life. Relationships create a complex web of obligations and responsibilities, which is how we extract meaning.

When you have an obligation, this is actually quite flattering, if you stop and think about it. Even what you may consider to be a slightly annoying or boring task at work, is actually deep. Someone, somewhere, values your input and your work and has asked you to do something. They haven't asked someone else — they asked *you.*
