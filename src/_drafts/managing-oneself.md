We live in unprecedented times. The global population has exploded, and so has the world's wealth. With this comes an unparalleled wealth of opportunities for those that choose to take them.

![](/media/2022/09/population-growth-1024x638.png)

For most of human history, population growth from generation to generation was not noticeable and did not impact the level of opportunities and challenges an individual faced.

Today, with the advent of technology, we live in a highly scalable society. Your knowledge and skills can create an outsized impact, but this also means that you are now competing against the best — regardless of where they live.
