Like any organization, UNDP has its jargon and acronyms. I thought I would create a place to set myself a reminder of what everything means.

-   **RR —** Regional Representation
-   **CO —** Country Office, there are 172 (?) country offices.
-   **CDO —** Chief Digital Office
-   **DT —** Digital Transformation
-   **RB —** Regional Bureau, there are x of these.
    -   **RBLAC —** Regional Bureau for Latin America and the Caribbean 
-   BERA
-   ITM — Information Technology Management
